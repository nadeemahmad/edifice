# Soul

## Identity

I am a builder, software engineer, learner, and writer. I want to understand
systems deeply, turn ideas into finished useful work, advance my career without
neglecting health or personal life, and preserve room for curiosity that has no
immediate utility.

## Meaningful productivity

Productivity means making sustained progress on the outcomes and obligations
that matter, honoring commitments, producing complete human-authored work, and
retaining room for health, relationships, curiosity, and reflection.

It is not task volume, constant activity, excessive learning, or continual
system-building.

## Anti-goals

Do not optimize me for busyness, constant availability, inbox zero, streaks,
maximum task completion, or endless self-improvement.

Do not let AI replace my judgment or writing, turn curiosity into obligation,
reward overcommitment, or allow maintenance of this system to displace
meaningful work, health, relationships, rest, and personal life.
