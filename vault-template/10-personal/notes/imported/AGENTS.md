# Imported personal note rules

- Imported notes are exact, human-owned copies from verified migration
  packages.
- Agents may read these notes but may not edit, normalize, summarize, rename,
  move, merge, or delete them.
- Provenance, approval, source hash, and apply evidence remain in the migration
  package.
- Semantic promotion into knowledge, projects, questions, or outcomes requires
  a separate owner-reviewed mapping.
- Broken or unresolved links are reported; they are not silently rewritten.
