# Personal migration rules

- Source systems and raw migration packages are read-only.
- Preserve source paths, identifiers, timestamps, modes, hashes, attachments,
  configuration, and validation evidence.
- Keep raw packages outside the live vault and normal vault search.
- Do not assume source folders are appropriate Edifice destinations.
- Every proposed mapping begins as `review`.
- Do not create live notes from a mapping without an approved apply step.
- Do not delete, rename, mutate, or retire a source account.
- Keep personal source material away from work workers and work storage.
