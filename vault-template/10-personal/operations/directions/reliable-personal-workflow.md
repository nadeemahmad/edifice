# Reliable Personal Workflow

## Direction

Use a small dependable system to turn chosen ideas into completed work without
losing context or letting system maintenance become the work.

## Why it matters

The system should reduce reconstruction, forgotten commitments, and avoidable
administration while protecting judgment and authorship.

## What this should not become

An endless configuration project or a substitute for completing real work.
