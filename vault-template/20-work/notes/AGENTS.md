# Human work-note rules

- The owner writes and controls every note in this directory.
- `edifice meeting start` and `edifice meeting series` may create empty
  meeting-note shells on an exact owner request.
- Recurring meeting commands may update only the marked history block in a
  standing series note.
- Agents may read an explicitly relevant note through an approved work context.
- Agents may not edit a note without exact owner approval.
- Suggestions and extracted operational items belong in the applicable
  `20-work/ai` or `20-work/operations` note.
- Meeting notes must retain their classification and work boundary.
